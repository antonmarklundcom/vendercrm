## Decisions and deviations

1. **`/contracts` gets a nav entry; `/contracts/templates` doesn't.** The
   former had no path to it at all outside a specific deal/quote/contact;
   the latter is one click from the former (already linked), so a second
   nav entry for it would be clutter for a sub-page already reachable.
2. **`businessName` and `neverPromise` stay on the AI settings form.**
   Unlike `about`/`tone`/`hours`, `resolveAiConfig` actually falls back to
   these two from `settings.ai` when a tenant has no profile row yet — they
   are not dead the way the other three are.
3. **K3 skipped, not attempted.** Its own prompt names K2 as a hard
   dependency and instructs skipping with a logged gap if K2 hasn't merged
   by the time the lane reaches it; K2 (wave 2 lane 1) had not merged.
