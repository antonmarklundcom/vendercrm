# Claude Design prompt — clientes.com.py home page, 3 directions (Fable-led)

Copy everything below the line into Claude Design.

---

I run **clientes.com.py** (our CRM product lives at crm.clientes.com.py). I want a new home page design, and I want you — Fable — to take the lead on it. Explore **three genuinely different design directions**, delivered as **three separate files**, one per direction. Each file gets a **desktop** artboard and a **mobile** artboard of the home page, a few key sections each (not the whole page). I'm giving you the brief, not the blueprint — the concepts, the craft, and almost every decision below are yours.

## The one thing that has to come through

**We get clients to businesses.** That's the whole pitch, and a visitor should get it in three seconds. This is not a software product page and must not read like a "buy our CRM" SaaS landing page.

How we do it is secondary and can surface as the toolkit behind the promise — digital marketing, automation, AI, our own CRM, and whatever else genuinely belongs there (websites, WhatsApp sales flows, follow-up systems...). Don't force a checklist; mention what earns its place.

Our customers are established business owners and sales managers. They care about more customers and an orderly sales process, not feature lists. Speak to that outcome with proof and confidence, and make it easy to start a conversation.

Market context, for your judgment: Spanish (Paraguay) for the mockup copy — the live site is trilingual (es/en/sv) so keep copy short and layouts tolerant of longer strings if that informs your layout choices. WhatsApp is the dominant contact channel in this market.

## What's yours to decide

Essentially everything. That includes, but isn't limited to:

- The three concepts themselves — what makes each one a distinct point of view, and what to name them
- Color, type, imagery/illustration style, motion cues, layout system
- Which sections each direction needs and in what order, including the hero
- Every headline and line of supporting copy, in service of the message above
- How "we get clients to businesses" gets expressed visually, not just stated
- The primary CTA and how WhatsApp shows up
- How mobile relates to desktop — reflowed, or its own mobile-first take
- Whether and how a CRM screenshot, illustration, or any other proof device belongs

If something in this brief feels like it's boxing you in more than it's helping, use your judgment and go with what makes the strongest design — just note the deviation in that file's intro.

## The few things I do need

- Three separate files, named by concept (e.g. `01-<concept-name>`).
- Each file: one desktop artboard and one mobile artboard, both fully designed (not a shrunk desktop).
- A short note at the top of each file — what the concept is, who it's for, why it fits the message.
- Real-feeling content — no lorem ipsum, no "Feature 1 / Feature 2" filler. Plausible placeholder numbers/names are fine if marked as such.
- No default SaaS tropes as the centerpiece — no dashboard-screenshot hero, no giant logo wall, no pricing table on the home page.
- Keep it usable: accessible contrast, real tap targets, and layouts that don't rely on heavy imagery to carry the page — this audience is often on mobile, slower connections.

## When you're done

Give me a short comparison of the three directions and your own recommendation for which to build first — but finish all three so I can actually choose between them.
