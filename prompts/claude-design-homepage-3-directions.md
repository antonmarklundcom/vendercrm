# Claude Design prompt — VenderCRM home page, 3 directions

Copy everything below the line into Claude Design.

---

I run VenderCRM (vendercrm.com). I want a new home page design and I want you to explore **three genuinely different design directions**, delivered as **three separate files**, one per direction. Each file must contain a **desktop** artboard and a **mobile** artboard of the home page, with a few key sections (not the whole page). Don't make any of the three a minor variation of another — they should feel like they were made by three different studios.

## Who we are (the message that matters)

VenderCRM's job is simple: **we get clients to businesses.** That is the headline idea, the thing a visitor should understand in three seconds. We are not a software product page and this must not read like a "buy our CRM" SaaS landing page.

How we do it is secondary, but it can be mentioned as the toolkit behind the promise: digital marketing, automation, AI, a CRM (our own), and anything else that naturally belongs in that toolkit (websites, WhatsApp sales flows, follow-up systems, etc. — use your judgment, don't force a list).

Our customers are established businesses — owners and sales managers who care about more customers and an orderly sales process, not about features. The home page should speak to that outcome, with proof and confidence, and make it easy to start a conversation with us.

Language for the mockups: Spanish (Paraguay). The real site is trilingual (es / en / sv), so keep copy short and layouts tolerant of longer strings. The primary contact channel in this market is WhatsApp — treat it as a first-class call to action, not a footer link.

## What you decide (please don't ask me)

For **each** of the three directions, you choose:

- The concept and personality (e.g. one could be bold and editorial, one warm and human, one precise and technical — those are just examples, pick your own three and name them)
- Color palette, typography, imagery style or illustration style, use of motion cues
- Which sections to show (aim for 3–5 per direction: a hero is required, the rest is up to you — proof/results, how we get clients, services toolkit, a case or testimonial, a CTA block, whatever serves the concept)
- Headline and supporting copy for every section, written to the message above
- How the "we get clients to businesses" promise is expressed visually
- The hero's primary CTA and how WhatsApp appears
- How desktop and mobile relate (same content re-flowed, or a different mobile-first take — your call, but both must be real, finished layouts, not a scaled-down desktop)

## Constraints

- Three files. Name them by direction, e.g. `01-<concept-name>`, `02-<concept-name>`, `03-<concept-name>`.
- In each file: one desktop artboard (1440 wide) and one mobile artboard (390 wide), stacked or side by side.
- At the top of each file, a short note (2–4 lines) explaining the concept, who it's for, and why it fits the message. I'll use these notes to compare.
- Real-looking content, no lorem ipsum, no "Feature 1 / Feature 2" placeholders. Numbers and client names may be plausible placeholders, marked as such in the note.
- Accessible contrast, mobile tap targets, and fast-feeling layouts (this market is mostly mobile on slow connections — heavy full-bleed imagery should earn its place).
- No generic SaaS tropes as the main event: no dashboard-screenshot hero, no "trusted by 10,000 companies" logo wall, no pricing table on the home page. A CRM screenshot may appear inside a section if it supports the "how" story.

## Delivery

When done, give me a one-paragraph comparison of the three directions and a recommendation for which to build first — but keep all three complete so I can choose.
